Home

Pale Horse Chess Club is a new, adults-only (and very unserious) chess club currently seeking members in the Kansas City area. The plan is to meet once per week to play chess and chat in either Lees Summit or Overland Park. If you're cool and interested, let me know by submitting this form or by sending a message via Instagram.

I'll be in touch once we have enough members to get this thing going.
